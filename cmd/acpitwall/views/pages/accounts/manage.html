Content downloads

Who may download cars, tracks, setups and car skins (the pictures shown on the pages stay visible). Content Manager gets signed, time limited skin links, so it keeps working when downloads are restricted.

Teams

Drivers can create teams, join them and register for championships with them. Accounts with write access can always create teams and manage every team.

0 is the default of 3, -1 is unlimited.

Car skins

Accounts can upload skins for cars and keep them private, share them with a team or make them global. Accounts with write access always upload global skins. The limits protect the disk: there is no unlimited setting, 0 uses the default shown in brackets.

An upload is refused if less than this would remain free on the disk.
